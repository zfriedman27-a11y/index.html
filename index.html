Before You Buy Checklist

Cash Flo does not say “buy” or “don’t buy.” It helps users slow down and ask better questions.

Before buying any stock, ask:

  • Do I understand what this company or ETF does?
  • Am I okay with the risks I’m taking?
  • Did I compare it to VOO, SPY, QQQ, or VTI?
  • Am I putting too much money into one idea?
  • Is this long-term thinking or hype?
