Practice first

Use watchlists, charts, and checklists before making real decisions in your own brokerage account.

Before investing, ask yourself:

  • Do I understand what this company or ETF actually does?
  • Am I okay with the risks I'm taking?
  • Is this a long-term idea or am I chasing hype?
  • Did I compare this to a broad ETF like VOO, SPY, or VTI?
  • Am I putting too much money into one stock?
  • Do I understand that Cash Flo is educational only?
